## Page 1: StockSaathi
Never run out. Never overstock.
Inventory and auto-reorder for kirana stores.
Indore | Pre-seed | November 2026
Rohan Jain, CEO | Aarti Choudhary, CTO

## Page 2: The problem
A typical kirana stocks 1,200 to 2,500 SKUs and tracks them in a notebook or memory.
Stock-outs on fast movers lose an estimated 6% to 9% of sales.
Owners place orders with 8 to 15 distributors over phone calls and WhatsApp.
Dead stock ties up ₹40,000 to ₹1 L of working capital per store.

## Page 3: The product
Barcode and voice-based stock entry in Hindi.
Sales sync from UPI and billing to update stock automatically.
Reorder suggestions based on 8 weeks of sell-through.
One-tap order to the right distributor, with order status tracking.
Price ₹700 a month per store.

## Page 4: Why now
Most kirana owners now run their counter on a smartphone.
UPI has digitised the sales side; the stock side is still manual.
Distributors want digital order books to plan routes.

## Page 5: Market size
TAM: 1.3 Cr kirana stores × ₹8,400 a year = ₹10,920 Cr
SAM: 18 L stores in Tier 2 cities of central and west India × ₹8,400 a year = ₹1,512 Cr
SOM: 2% of SAM in 5 years = ₹30 Cr
