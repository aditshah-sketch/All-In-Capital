## Page 2: The problem
A ₹300 Cr manufacturer receives 3,000 to 6,000 supplier invoices a month.
AP teams of 8 to 15 people key them in, three-way match against PO and GRN, and chase approvals on email.
Average invoice processing cost: ₹310 per invoice (our study of 12 plants, 2025).
Late payments cost early-payment discounts of 1% to 2% and strain MSME suppliers.

## Page 3: The product
Invoice capture from e-invoice portal, email and WhatsApp.
Automatic three-way match with PO and GRN from the customer's ERP.
Approval workflows on mobile for plant heads.
Payment batch files pushed to the company's bank.
Live in 4 to 6 weeks; connectors for the 5 most common mid-market ERPs.

## Page 4: Why now

MSME 45-day payment rule makes late payment a tax cost.
CFOs at mid-size plants want headcount-flat growth.
