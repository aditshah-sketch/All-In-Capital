## Page 2: The problem
India has lakhs of kilometres of transmission and distribution lines.
Most inspection is still done by linemen on foot or by helicopter.
Faults like cracked insulators and hot joints are missed until they cause an outage.
Outages cost utilities and industry thousands of crores every year.

## Page 3: The solution
Autonomous quadcopter that flies along the line with no pilot input.
Thermal plus RGB camera with onboard AI fault detection.
Fault report with GPS tag sent to the utility dashboard in real time.
One drone can inspect up to 40 km of line a day (design target).

## Page 4: Technology
Proprietary vision model trained on 18,000 labelled images.
Edge inference on a compact GPU module, under 120 ms per frame.
Custom flight controller firmware for line-following.
Patent application in preparation.
