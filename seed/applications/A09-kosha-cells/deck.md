## Page 2: The problem
E-rickshaw operators replace lead-acid packs every 12 to 15 months.
Lithium packs last longer but cost ₹85,000 to ₹1.1 L and degrade fast in 45 degree summers.
Lithium cell imports expose operators to price swings and supply shocks.
Pack fires in heat have made fleet owners cautious.

## Page 3: The solution
48 V sodium-ion pack designed for e-rickshaws.
Target price: ₹55,000 per pack, about 35% below comparable lithium packs.
Rated for 2,500 cycles; safe to ship at zero charge.
Our own BMS tuned for sodium-ion voltage curves.

## Page 4: Technology
Lab packs: 1,500 cycles at 45 degrees C with 88% capacity retained (Oct 2026).
Thermal runaway onset 40 degrees C higher than our lithium benchmark in lab tests.
Cells sourced from 2 qualified suppliers; pack, BMS and thermal design in-house.
2 patent applications filed (pack thermal design, BMS state-of-charge method).
