## Page 1: PantryWise
Your kitchen, refilled before you run out.
Monthly grocery-staples subscription for urban households.

## Page 2: The problem
Urban families buy the same 25 to 40 staples every month: atta, rice, dal, oil, sugar, tea.
They still shop for them in 6 to 9 separate orders a month.
Quick-commerce prices for staples run 8% to 12% above modern trade.
Running out of oil or atta on a weekday means a rushed, expensive top-up.
Survey of 410 Bengaluru households (Jun 2026): 71% ran out of at least one staple in the last month.

## Page 3: The solution
A smart monthly basket that learns what each household consumes.
Auto-built list from the first 2 orders; edit anything before the cut-off.
One scheduled delivery a month, plus one free mid-month top-up slot.
Prices locked for the month, sourced directly from 3 distributors.
Average basket: ₹2,150 a month.

## Page 4: Why now
App-based grocery is now habit for metro households.
UPI AutoPay mandates make recurring billing simple and cheap.
Quick commerce has trained users on convenience but not on planned buying.
Distributors are looking for predictable, pre-committed volumes.
