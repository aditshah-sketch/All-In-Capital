## Page 2: The problem
1.3 Cr kirana stores need short-term stock credit before festivals and month-end.
Banks ask for ITRs and collateral most kirana owners do not have.
Local moneylenders charge 3% to 5% a month.
Distributor credit is limited to one brand at a time.

## Page 3: The solution
An app that offers loans of ₹10,000 to ₹75,000 for 30 to 90 days.
Approval in under 10 minutes using UPI QR transaction history.
Disbursed and repaid through UPI; daily auto-collect option.
Loans are originated and held by our NBFC partner.

## Page 4: How it works
1. Kirana owner downloads the app and links UPI QR.
2. Our model scores 90 days of transactions.
3. NBFC partner approves and disburses.
4. Repayment via daily UPI auto-collect.
We earn a sourcing fee plus a share of interest from the NBFC.
