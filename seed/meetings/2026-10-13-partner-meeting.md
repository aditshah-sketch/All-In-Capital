## What decides a deal

Riya asked for a plain answer on what matters most when the sector looks attractive. Kushal went back through the year. Charge Chalo had a great sector story and great founders, structure was wrong. Boli Seekho is consumer tech, a category we have been nervous about, and we backed it because Anjali ran WhatsApp classes for 6 months and knew every cohort. Ledgerline sat in our favourite category and we passed on how the founder behaved. "Founder behaviour beats the sector label. Every time we have argued from the sector we have been wrong, and every time we have argued from what the founder did, we have been mostly right."

Aditya added that this is why he wants the scoring to stay evidence-first: what the founder did before the product, which numbers they have, and how they answer the hard question.
