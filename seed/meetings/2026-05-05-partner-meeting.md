Crypto is outside the thesis anyway. Decision: pass. Riya to keep Neha's details.

**Lending pipeline.** Two new lending applications came in this month. Riya asked how to screen them quickly. Kushal pointed to Mitti Credit versus QuickPaisa. "Pradeep and Meenal spent 13 months in villages before lending and carry 20% first-loss. Rohan told us defaults were the NBFC's problem. The founder has to own the hardest risk in the business. In lending that is credit, full stop." Aditya added that the same test works outside lending: in deeptech it is the technology risk, in consumer it is repeat. Riya will ask both lending founders who carries first-loss and for their vintage curve before booking any call.
