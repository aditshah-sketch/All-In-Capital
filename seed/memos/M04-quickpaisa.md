## What it does
QuickPaisa is an app that gives instant personal loans of ₹5,000 to ₹50,000 to salaried workers in tier-2 cities, with a 3 to 6 month tenure. Underwriting uses bank statement parsing and a bureau pull. All loans are booked on the balance sheet of an NBFC partner, and QuickPaisa earns a sourcing fee of 3% plus a share of interest. Disbursal is ₹1.8 Cr a month across about 6,000 loans. They acquire users through Google and affiliate apps at about ₹650 per disbursed loan. The raise is ₹3.5 Cr, mainly for marketing and tech hires.

## Founder assessment
Rohan Sabharwal (ex product manager at a payments company, 5 years) and Aakash Tiwari (engineering). The app is well built, with disbursal in under 9 minutes. Rohan knows the funnel conversion at every step. When Riya asked about the 90-day default rate he said it was "around 7 or 8%" and added that "defaults are the NBFC's problem, we just source." He did not know the vintage curve or the default rate by acquisition channel. There is no first-loss guarantee in place and he did not intend to offer one. Aakash said collections are handled by the NBFC's agency.

## Risks and open questions
Unsecured small-ticket personal lending has a history of fast growth followed by fast credit losses. If the NBFC partner sees default above its threshold it can cut the sourcing line overnight, and QuickPaisa has no other partner signed. RBI digital lending guidelines limit how fees and first-loss can be structured, and collections conduct is heavily scrutinised. CAC of ₹650 against a sourcing fee of roughly ₹450 on an average ₹15,000 loan means each loan loses money unless the customer borrows again.

## Why we passed
The founder does not own the credit risk, the NBFC does, and he was comfortable with that. In lending, credit is the hardest risk, and a founder who treats defaults as a partner's problem will optimise for volume. Compare Mitti Credit, where the founders carry first-loss and know every bad village. A good app was not enough. Unit economics without repeat borrowing are also negative. Pass. We also noted that Rohan had no plan for a second NBFC partner if the first one cut the line. Riya sent a short note explaining the credit point.
