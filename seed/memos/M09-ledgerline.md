## What it does
Ledgerline reconciles GST input credit for small manufacturers and traders. It pulls purchase registers from billing software, matches them against supplier filings on the GST portal and lists mismatches with a WhatsApp nudge to the supplier. Pricing is ₹1,500 a month per GSTIN. They have 210 paying GSTINs, mostly in Ludhiana and Rajkot, at ₹3.2 L MRR. Monthly logo churn is 3.5%. About 40% of customers came through 6 chartered accountant firms that resell it. They are raising ₹2.5 Cr to build an e-invoicing module and hire 4 salespeople.

## Founder assessment
Karan Gill (CA, 6 years in audit at a mid-size firm) and Sumit Arora (engineer, built the matching engine). Karan knows the customer problem in detail and can quote the average blocked credit per customer (₹2.8 L a year). Sales numbers were clear. When Kushal asked how they would respond if one of the large, well-funded accounting suites shipped the same reconciliation feature free inside its plan, Karan said the question "shows you have not understood the product," that "nobody can copy our matching logic," and moved to the next slide. Asked again later, he said the large suites are "too slow to matter." Sumit was quieter and said the matching took them 8 months. Neither discussed distribution through CA firms as a moat.

## Risks and open questions
Reconciliation is a feature that GST portal improvements or large accounting suites could commoditise. Churn of 3.5% a month means roughly a third of customers leave in a year. CA reseller channel gives distribution but CA firms can switch tools for their whole client base at once. Price point of ₹1,500 limits room for a field sales team. E-invoicing module puts them in direct competition with many established vendors. Two of the CA resellers bring half that channel.

## Why we passed
The business is real and the numbers were fine. What decided it was how the founder handled the hardest question. Copy risk is the obvious weak spot, and Karan got defensive twice instead of thinking it through. Defensiveness on a weak spot is information about how he will handle bad news from customers and from us. Pass. Sumit was more open than Karan, and we noted that. Riya's first founder score of 4 out of 5 was lowered after the partner discussion. We would still take a call from Sumit on a future company.
