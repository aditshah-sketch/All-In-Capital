## What it does
Pervalent makes anion exchange membranes for small electrolysers that produce green hydrogen, and a variant for industrial effluent treatment. Membranes today are imported at roughly ₹38,000 per square metre. Pervalent's pilot line in Navi Mumbai makes 20 sq m a month at a material cost of about ₹9,000 per sq m. The hydrogen use needs a durability certification (2,000 hours of stack testing at a government-recognised lab), which takes 14 to 18 months. The effluent membrane needs only a 4-month performance trial with the buyer. They are raising ₹3 Cr for a second pilot line, test lab fees and three hires.

## Founder assessment
Dr Kavita Iyer (PhD in polymer chemistry, 9 years at a national lab working on ion exchange materials) and Sandeep Nair (chemical engineer, 11 years scaling coating lines at a specialty films company). They brought a one-page milestone plan: effluent trial with a Vapi dye unit (signed, starts in 6 weeks), first effluent revenue by month 7, hydrogen stack test starting month 4, certification by month 20, and 2 electrolyser OEM letters of intent already in hand. Each milestone has a cost attached. Kavita showed 1,100 hours of in-house stack data and was open that conductivity drops 6% after 800 hours.

## Risks and open questions
Certification is long and can fail, and a fail at hour 1,800 would cost about 8 months. Hydrogen demand in India depends on subsidy-led electrolyser orders, which may arrive slower than announced. The effluent market is smaller and price-sensitive, average contract perhaps ₹25 L a year. Scaling from 20 to 200 sq m a month needs a coating line costing roughly ₹1.2 Cr that is not in this round. Two founders, no commercial hire yet. Global membrane makers could cut prices for Indian OEMs.

## Why we invested
This is the kind of deeptech we still want: technical founders with a decade in the field, and a milestone path through a long certification where every step has a date and a cost. The effluent product gives revenue before certification, so the company is not 20 months of pure burn. Aditya led the deal and owns the quarterly milestone review. We invested ₹2 Cr of ₹3 Cr. The open admission about the 6% conductivity drop counted in their favour, not against.
