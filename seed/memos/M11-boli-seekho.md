## What it does
Boli Seekho teaches spoken English through Hindi, Bengali, Odia and Telugu, with 10-minute daily voice lessons built around job situations (retail floor, delivery, hotel front desk, call centre). Speech scoring runs on-device. Pricing is ₹149 a month or ₹999 a year. There are 4.2 L downloads, 61,000 monthly active users and 8,400 paying subscribers, giving ₹9.8 L MRR. Paid M6 retention is 34% for the Aug 2025 cohort, and free-to-paid conversion is 6.1%. 64% of users are outside the top 8 cities. They are raising ₹3 Cr for two more languages and a placement partnership.

## Founder assessment
Anjali Deshmukh (taught English at a government ITI in Nagpur for 5 years, then product at an edtech company) and Ravi Patnaik (ML engineer, speech models). Anjali ran WhatsApp voice-note classes for 400 ITI students for 6 months before the app, and the lesson structure comes from that. She gave M1, M3 and M6 by cohort, and explained why the Oct cohort is 4 points lower (a festive season discount brought lower-intent users). Ravi showed the speech model's accuracy by language and admitted Odia is weakest at 81%.

## Risks and open questions
Consumer edtech in India has a history of high spend and churn. ₹149 a month is a low price that needs scale. Larger language apps could add Indian-language-to-English tracks. Placement partnerships are unproven and can turn into a lead-gen business. Odia model quality could hurt reviews in that market. Android device fragmentation affects on-device speech scoring for phones under ₹8,000. Paid acquisition today costs ₹410 per subscriber on Meta and is rising. Placement partners have not yet been signed, only spoken to. App store reviews are 4.3 overall but 3.8 for Odia.

## Why we invested
34% paid M6 retention is real repeat for a consumer app at this price, and Anjali knows her cohorts as well as any consumer brand founder we have backed. The groundwork came first through the WhatsApp classes. A consumer tech label alone would not have got us here; founder behaviour did. We invested ₹1.7 Cr. Anjali volunteering the Oct cohort dip before we asked counted strongly in her favour. Riya tracks the monthly cohort file and Ravi's Odia model accuracy.
