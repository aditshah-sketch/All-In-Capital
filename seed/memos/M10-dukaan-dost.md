## What it does
Dukaan Dost is an Android app for kirana stores that tracks inventory from billing scans and auto-generates reorder lists, which it sends to the store's chosen distributors on WhatsApp. It works offline and in Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati. Pricing is ₹299 a month, with a free tier for stores under ₹3 L monthly sales. There are 1,850 paying stores across Indore, Ujjain and Bhopal, MRR ₹5.5 L. Store-level M6 retention on the paid plan is 71%. Distributors also pay ₹2,000 a month for an order dashboard, with 38 signed. They are raising ₹2.2 Cr.

## Founder assessment
Manoj Agarwal (grew up in his family's kirana in Ujjain, then 5 years in FMCG distribution sales) and Pooja Sethi (Android engineer, 6 years). Before writing code they sat in 60 kiranas for two weeks each and logged stockouts by hand on paper. They found an average of 23 stockouts a week per store, and the first version only did reorder lists in a notebook-style screen. Manoj knows the cost to onboard a store (₹640 including a field visit) and the reasons for each of the last 20 churned stores. Pooja showed the offline sync design and crash rate (0.4%).

## Risks and open questions
Kirana SaaS has many attempts behind it and willingness to pay is low. ₹299 a month leaves thin room for field onboarding. Large B2B distribution platforms could offer similar tools free to lock in ordering. Expansion outside Madhya Pradesh means new languages and distributor networks. Distributor revenue depends on store density in a town. Team of 12, with no sales leader yet. Field onboarding depends on 5 people who could leave. Distributor churn has not been measured yet because the first contracts are only 7 months old. Android phone quality in small towns affects scanning speed.

## Why we invested
Customer groundwork came before product: 60 kiranas sat with and stockouts logged by hand before a line of code. That shows in 71% paid M6 retention, which is real repeat for a small-business tool. Unglamorous, small-town, and exactly what keeps working for us. We invested ₹1.5 Cr. Neel tracks the monthly cohort file. Manoj knowing the reason behind each of the last 20 churned stores told us he owns the customer risk himself. Distributor revenue is upside we did not price in.
