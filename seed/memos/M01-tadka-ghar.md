## What it does
Tadka Ghar sells regional spice blends (Kolhapuri, Chettinad, Malvani, Bihari sattu masala) in 100 g and 250 g pouches. Sourcing is direct from 14 farmer groups in Sangli and Guntur, grinding is done at a contract unit in Pune. Current run rate is ₹22 L a month, with 58% from its own website, 27% from two quick-commerce apps and 15% from 40 modern trade stores in Pune and Mumbai. Average order value on the website is ₹540. The team is 9 people. They are raising ₹2.5 Cr to add 6 SKUs, open Bengaluru modern trade and build a small in-house grinding line to cut contract costs by roughly 4 margin points.

## Founder assessment
Shalini Kothari (ex category manager at a large FMCG company, 7 years) and Abhay Kulkarni (ops, ran his family's wholesale spice business in Sangli). Shalini answered every number without opening a laptop. Website cohort repeat for the Jul 2024 cohort: M1 31%, M3 42%, M6 51% cumulative. Older cohorts are within 3 points. Gross margin 61% on website, 44% on quick commerce after platform fees. CAC by channel: ₹310 Meta, ₹190 Google search, ₹85 from WhatsApp referrals. She volunteered that quick commerce repeat is not measurable because the platforms do not share customer IDs. Abhay knows procurement cost per kg by district and by season.

## Risks and open questions
Spices are crowded and big FMCG brands can discount hard in modern trade. Quick commerce share is rising and at 44% margin it pulls the blended margin down. In-house grinding adds capex of about ₹40 L and an FSSAI licence change, which could slip by a quarter. Founder pair has not hired a marketing lead yet and Shalini runs paid ads herself. Bengaluru modern trade needs a distributor they have not signed. We did not verify the cohort data ourselves beyond a Shopify export for two months.

## Why we invested
Shalini was the clearest example we have seen of a consumer founder who knows her repeat and margin by cohort and by channel without being prompted. M6 repeat above 50% on spices is real habit, not a promotion spike. CAC on referrals is under a quarter of paid, which means growth does not depend on Meta prices. We put in ₹1.8 Cr of the ₹2.5 Cr round. Kushal flagged the quick commerce margin drag as the thing to watch at the 6-month review.
