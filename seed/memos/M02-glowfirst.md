## What it does
GlowFirst sells vitamin C serums, sunscreen and a niacinamide face wash to women aged 18 to 28, mostly in metros. It is a white-label model: formulations come from a contract manufacturer in Baddi, GlowFirst owns branding and marketing. Revenue is ₹35 L a month, up from ₹8 L a year ago. About 72% of sales come from paid social (Instagram and Meta reels with micro-influencers), 18% from a marketplace and 10% from organic website traffic. Average order value is ₹649. They are raising ₹3 Cr, of which ₹2 Cr is earmarked for performance marketing over the next 12 months.

## Founder assessment
Varun Malhotra (ex growth lead at a fashion e-commerce company, 4 years) and Tanya Bedi (brand and content). Both are sharp on creative. Varun can recite ROAS by ad set, last week's CPM and the top 5 performing reels. When Riya asked for repeat rate by monthly cohort, Varun said they "do not track it that way" and that overall about "a good chunk" of buyers come back. He could not give M3 or M6 repeat for any cohort. Gross margin was quoted as 70%, but that was before marketplace fees and returns. CAC was given as a blended ₹420, not split by channel. Tanya said they planned to set up cohort tracking after the round.

## Risks and open questions
With 72% of revenue from paid social, any rise in CPM goes straight to contribution margin. Skincare has low switching costs and many white-label brands use the same Baddi manufacturers, so product differentiation is thin. Without repeat data we cannot tell whether the ₹35 L is a retained customer base or a new-buyer treadmill. Returns were estimated at 8% but not evidenced. The round is mostly marketing spend, which scales the current funnel rather than testing whether customers stay. Inventory of 11 SKUs ties up about ₹40 L.

## Why we passed
We could not score the thing that matters most in a consumer brand. Repeat by cohort was missing and the founder did not see it as a gap. A missing number is not neutral for us, it lowers the score, because a founder who cannot give repeat is usually buying the same customer every month. Paid social dependence above 70% made it worse. We told Varun we would look again if he came back with six months of cohort data.
