## What it does
MeetRecord records sales calls on video call tools, phone and WhatsApp calls, transcribes them in English, Hindi and Hinglish, and writes a summary with next steps into the customer's CRM. It also flags calls where pricing or a competitor came up. Customers are inside sales teams of 10 to 80 people at Indian SMB software and education companies. Pricing is ₹1,200 per seat per month. They have 74 paying companies and 1,920 seats, MRR ₹23 L. Net revenue retention over 12 months is 118%. They are raising ₹6 Cr in a seed round led by another fund.

## Founder assessment
Siddharth Menon (ran an inside sales team of 60 at an edtech company for 4 years) and Aisha Qureshi (speech and NLP engineer, 7 years). Siddharth started by manually listening to and summarising calls for 3 sales teams for 2 months to learn what managers actually read. Aisha knows transcription word error rate by language (Hinglish 11%) and cost per call hour (₹4.10). They gave CAC (₹62,000 per company), payback (5 months) and churn by company size without notes. Siddharth answered the question about global competitors directly and named two features he expected them to copy first.

## Risks and open questions
Call recording and summarisation is a fast-moving space and global tools are adding Indian languages. Speech model costs could rise if they rely on third-party APIs for some languages. Consent and recording laws for phone calls vary and the upcoming data protection rules add obligations. SMB customers churn when they cut sales teams. A cheque from us would be a minority in a round led by another fund, which limits influence. Two customers make up 14% of MRR.

## Why we invested
Founder owns the customer problem (he ran the team), groundwork came before product, and the numbers were fluent. 118% net revenue retention shows teams add seats. Seed is at the edge of our stage, but the entry price was fair. We took ₹2 Cr of the ₹6 Cr round. Kushal holds the relationship. Siddharth's direct answer on global competitors mattered to us. MeetRecord is also tagged for overlap checks against new applications in call recording and sales notes.
