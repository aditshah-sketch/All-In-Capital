## What it does
Charge Chalo runs an owned fleet of electric autos and e-scooters for last-mile rides in Jaipur, booked through its own app. Drivers are salaried, vehicles are owned by the company, and charging is done at 3 company depots. Current fleet is 140 vehicles doing about 3,100 rides a day at an average fare of ₹58. Vehicle utilisation is 9.5 hours a day. The plan is to grow to 2,500 vehicles across 3 cities, which by their own model needs about ₹60 Cr in vehicles and depots before contribution margin per vehicle turns positive after depreciation. They are asking for ₹8 Cr now as the first part of that.

## Founder assessment
Nikhil Rao (ran operations for a large ride-hailing company in Rajasthan for 5 years) and Farah Siddiqui (electrical engineer, built battery swapping depots at an EV maker). Both are excellent. Nikhil knew driver attrition by month (11%), rides per vehicle per day by zone and the exact hour when demand peaks. Farah explained battery degradation at 18 months and what it does to range. When asked about the funding need, they were direct that the company needs debt and equity of roughly ₹60 Cr before unit economics are visible, and showed the model.

## Risks and open questions
Asset-heavy model: each vehicle costs ₹2.4 L and depreciates over 4 years. Vehicle debt for an early company is expensive, around 14 to 16%. Contribution margin per vehicle after driver salary, charging and maintenance is ₹1,900 a month today before depreciation, negative after. Fare competition from aggregator apps using driver-owned vehicles. Battery replacement at year 3 is a large cost not yet incurred. Expansion to new cities restarts depot capex. Depot leases run 3 years with lock-ins.

## Why we passed
We pass on structure, not people. Nikhil and Farah are among the best operators we met this year. But a ₹60 Cr capital need before unit economics show does not fit a pre-seed cheque of up to $500K: our money would be a rounding error and our outcome would depend entirely on later rounds. Kushal asked Riya to keep in touch with both founders for any future, lighter company. Kushal wanted the reason recorded plainly so it is not read as a judgement on the team.
